# FinSight Product Design System

FinSight AI is an evidence-driven equity research workspace. This system turns
its engineering capabilities into a calm, search-first research product for
individual investors, interview reviewers, and research engineers.

## Product hierarchy

1. Search or select a company from the persistent shell.
2. Read identity, current quote, one price line, and essential metrics in 公司研究.
3. Generate and read the structured conclusion in AI 分析.
4. Inspect source material in 证据来源.
5. Review company disclosures and signals in 近期事件.
6. Maintain a focused company set in 关注列表.

# Voice and tone

- Sound like a careful research analyst, not a trading influencer.
- Prefer short declarative sentences and qualified conclusions.
- Use sentence case. Avoid exclamation marks and emoji.
- Never say “buy”, “sell”, “guaranteed”, or imply certain returns.
- Express uncertainty explicitly: “Evidence is limited”, “Based on the latest snapshot”.
- Format A-share symbols as `600519.SH`.
- Format prices with two decimals and always show a signed percentage change.
- Use Chinese for research explanations; keep standard market abbreviations when clearer.
